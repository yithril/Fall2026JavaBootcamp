# BigDecimal Practice Exercise: Restaurant Checkout Calculator

## Objective

Create a Java program that simulates a restaurant checkout system using `BigDecimal`.

Do NOT use:
- `double`
- `float`

---

# Starting Values

Use these values in your program:

| Name | Value |
|---|---|
| subtotal | 85.50 |
| tax rate | 6% |
| tip rate | 20% |

---

# Program Requirements and Business Logic

## 1. Apply Discount

If the subtotal is greater than `$50.00`:

- apply a 10% discount
- calculate the discounted subtotal

---

## 2. Calculate Tax

- tax is based on the discounted subtotal
- round tax to 2 decimal places

---

## 3. Calculate Tip

- tip is based on the discounted subtotal
- round tip to 2 decimal places

---

## 4. Calculate Final Total

The final total should include:
- discounted subtotal
- tax
- tip

Round the final total to 2 decimal places.

---

## 5. Print Receipt

Print:
- original subtotal
- discount amount
- discounted subtotal
- tax
- tip
- final total

Example format:

```text
Original Subtotal: $85.50
Discount: $8.55
Discounted Subtotal: $76.95
Tax: $4.62
Tip: $15.39
Final Total: $96.96
```
